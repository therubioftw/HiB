# Custom data in Bob

Take-home exercise for the Senior Product Manager, Core HR role at HiBob: a recommended product direction for configurable data structures.

- **Reading version** (each slide followed by its notes): `present.html?read`, also the site root
- **Presenting version**: `present.html` (arrow keys to move, **N** shows or hides the notes)
- **Prototype**: `prototype/`, a clickable mock-up of Bob with a step guide; people, numbers and AI answers are made up

Screenshots in `img/` are cropped from the HiBob demo environment shared for this exercise.
